Sidenote: Juggling is usually limited to one hit while the opponent is in the
air, however A.y gives a free juggle if the enemy is in the air. Dash Canceling
also gives a free juggle. Hypers can juggle if timed right. only C.a
can chain slightly, the rest can only be chained by linking. What can and can't
be linked is undetermined at the time.

F.A.Q.:
Q:"How is Sagat able to chain all that crap togethor and I can't?"
A:"He's not chaining at all, he's linking which is very possible but requires strict timing"

Q:"Does his pallette select work like Dudleys?"
