-This isnt supposed to be an exact replica of C.Akuma.
-I wanted him to play a bit more like ken with some of C.Akuma's Hypers.

========================
Basics
========================

x= Low Punch
Y= Medium Punch
Z= Hard Punch

A= Low Kick
B= Medium Kick
C= Hard Kick

Down, Up = Super Jump

Hold Forward = Forward Wakeup Roll 
Hold Back  = Backward Wakeup Roll 

========================

========================

[Kick Throw]
-Foward, a+b
